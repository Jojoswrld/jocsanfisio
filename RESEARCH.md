## Costa Rica market
- **License:** Colegio de Terapeutas de Costa Rica (CTCR). Its ethics code bans misleading ads and charging below the official fee schedule. Showing the CTCR code builds trust (Huli profiles show it). [CTCR ethics code](https://colegiodeterapeutas.cr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/CAR007-Codigo-de-Etica-profesional-Colegio-de-Terapeutas-de-Costa-Rica.pdf)
- **Minimum fees (CTCR, 2022):**
  - Clinic: ₡15,400 urban, ₡12,320 rural.
  - Home visit: ₡18,500 (up to 10 km), ₡20,500 (11–20 km), ₡25,700 (over 20 km).
  - [CTCR fee schedule](https://colegiodeterapeutas.cr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/8R18-Honorarios-Minimos-Profesionales-CTCR-V.02-COPIA-NO-CONTROLADA.pdf). Check whether there is a newer schedule.
- **Market prices:** about ₡20,000–35,000 per session in San José. Home visits run around ₡35,000. Packages are common, e.g. 6 sessions for ₡120,000. [SKÁL prices](https://skal.cr/terapia-fisica-precios/)
- **How people book:** WhatsApp first, then Instagram and Facebook, Google Maps and Waze, and the HuliHealth directory.
- **Money:** a factura electrónica is required. Health services carry 4% IVA, refunded on card payments. People pay by SINPE Móvil, card or cash. Private insurers (INS, BMI, Palig, Mapfre) cover physio through their networks. [Hacienda guide](https://www.hacienda.go.cr/docs/ServiciosSaludHumanaPrivada.pdf)
- **Privacy (Ley 8968):** health data is sensitive. Any form needs a consent checkbox and a privacy note.
- **Local competitor:** [erickfisiocr.com](https://erickfisiocr.com/), a sports physio in Ciudad Quesada and La Fortuna. It has booking and testimonials but no prices, no CTCR code and no English.
