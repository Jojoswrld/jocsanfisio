Questions patients ask

Five common doubts I explain on Instagram (in Spanish).

My knee cracks when I squat. Is that bad?It depends
What we know today
About 4 in 10 people have noisy knees, and many feel no pain. If it cracks without pain, keep exercising.
Message me if
The noise comes with pain, swelling, or your knee feels like it “catches”.
I sprained my ankle: ice and stay off it?Outdated
What we know today
Keeping still isn’t what helps most. With rehab exercises, the chance of spraining it again drops by about 40%.
Message me if
You sprain your ankle often or it feels like it “gives way”.
Is it normal to leak a little when I cough or jump?Not normal
What we know today
It’s very common after childbirth and in menopause, but it can get better. The first treatment is exercise, not surgery.
Message me if
You wear a pad “just in case” or stopped exercising because of it.
How many times can you stand up from a chair in 30 seconds?Try it at home
What we know today
It’s the same strength you use to get out of a car or climb stairs, and you can build it back at any age. Use a sturdy chair against the wall, with someone beside you.
Message me if
You couldn’t stand up without using your hands, or someone in your family fell this year.
My back hurts: should I stay in bed?Myth
What we know today
Days in bed make your back stiffer and weaker. Short walks several times a day and keeping your routine help more.
Message me if
The pain gets in the way of work or sleep. If you can’t control your bladder or you have a fever, see a doctor right away.
