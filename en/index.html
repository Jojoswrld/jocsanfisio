What do people ask him most?

Five questions he explains on Instagram (in Spanish), @jocsanfisio.

My knee cracks when I squat. Is that bad?

It depends

About 4 in 10 people have noisy knees, and many feel no pain. If it cracks without pain, keep exercising. Message him if it comes with pain, swelling, or the knee “catches”.

I sprained my ankle: ice and stay off it?

Outdated

Keeping still isn’t what helps most. With rehab exercises, the chance of spraining it again drops by about 40%.

Is it normal to leak a little when I cough or jump?

Not normal

It’s very common after childbirth and in menopause, but it can get better. The first treatment is exercise, not surgery.

How many times can you stand up from a chair in 30 seconds?

Try it at home

It’s the same strength you use to get out of a car or climb stairs, and you can build it back at any age. Use a sturdy chair against the wall, with someone beside you.

My back hurts: should I stay in bed?

Myth

Days in bed make your back stiffer. Short walks several times a day help more. If you can’t control your bladder or you have a fever, see a doctor right away.
